Intramuros

The Oldest District in Manila

Built in 1571 by the Spanish, Intramuros is the oldest district in Manila. Surrounded by thick walls that served as the center of Spanish colonial government in Asia. Today, it is a historic site full of old churches, museums, and cobblestone streets — perfect for riders who want to explore history.

📍
Location Manila

Distance 0km (already in Manila)
⏱️

🏍️
Road Type Paved, city roads
💡
Best Time December–February

Vigan Heritage Town

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Vigan is one of the best-preserved Spanish colonial towns in Asia. Calle Crisologo, full of ancestral houses and cobblestone streets, gives you the feeling of traveling back to the Spanish era. The kalesa rides and local delicacies like longganisa and empanada are also popular.

📍
Location Ilocos Sur
🛣️
Distance 408km from Manila
⏱️
Ride Time 7-8 hours
🏍️
Road Type NLEX + SCTEX + National Highway
💡
Best Time October–March
🅿️
Parking Public parking near the plaza

LUZON

Banaue Rice Terraces

The 8th Wonder of the World

Built over 2,000 years ago by the Ifugao, the Banaue Rice Terraces are one of the greatest engineering feats of ancient Filipinos. The terraces reach 1,500 meters high and cascade down the mountainside — a breathtaking sight for riders exploring the Cordillera region.

📍
Location Ifugao
🛣️
Distance 350km from Manila
⏱️
Ride Time 6-7 hours
🏍️
Road Type Halsema Highway (zigzag!)
💡
Best Time March–May
🅿️
Parking Parking at the Viewpoint

Sagada

Land of the Hanging Coffins

Known for the hanging coffins, Sumaguing Cave, and Kiltepan Peak sunrise, Sagada is a mystical destination in the Cordillera. Adventure riders will enjoy the winding roads getting here, while nature lovers will love the caves, waterfalls, and misty mountains.

📍
Location Mountain Province
🛣️
Distance 390km from Manila
⏱️
Ride Time 8-9 hours
🏍️
Road Type Halsema Highway + local roads
💡
Best Time October–February
🅿️
Parking Parking at town proper

Mayon Volcano

The World's Most Perfect Volcano

With its uniquely perfect cone shape, Mayon Volcano is one of the most recognizable natural landmarks in the Philippines. Located in Albay, Bicol Region, the area is full of adventure — from ATV trails to Cagsawa Ruins. The Bicol Express highway is one of the best motorcycle routes in the country.

📍
Location Albay, Bicol
🛣️
Distance 500km from Manila
⏱️
Ride Time 9-10 hours
🏍️
Road Type SLEX + NLEX + Bicol Express
💡
Best Time November–May
🅿️
Parking Parking at Cagsawa Ruins
