HELLO, I'M KERVY

Rider. Explorer. History Buff.

I only started riding in 2024, but it already changed how I see the Philippines. My first ride was to Dingalan — a break-in trip with my titas who also love riding. After that, I went back to Dingalan with a classmate, then again with my girlfriend. I want to ride farther, but budget for overnight trips is still tight. Still, I've already experienced San Juan, La Union with my girlfriend — we followed her family's outing, then pushed on to Baguio before heading home. Soon, I want to see more. I've also been a pahinante (passenger) for years, so I've already been to Bicol, Batangas, and many parts of Luzon. That's how I know how beautiful and fun riding really is.
