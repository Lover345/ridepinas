Luzon

Banaue Rice Terraces

The 8th Wonder of the World

Back to Destinations

📍 Quick Facts

Location: Ifugao
Distance: 350km from Manila
Ride Time: 6-7 hours
Road Type: Halsema Highway (zigzag!)
Best Time: March–May
Parking: Parking at the Viewpoint

History

Built over 2,000 years ago by the Ifugao, the Banaue Rice Terraces are one of the greatest engineering feats of ancient Filipinos. The terraces reach 1,500 meters high and cascade down the mountainside — a breathtaking sight for riders exploring the Cordillera region.

The terraces were carved into the mountains by hand, using just basic tools. The irrigation system channels water from the rainforests above through an intricate network of canals. Some of these systems are still in use today.

Why Visit?

  • See the 2,000-year-old rice terraces carved into the mountains
  • Trek to Batad Amphitheater — the most spectacular viewpoint
  • Learn about Ifugao culture and traditions
  • Visit the Banaue Rice Terraces Viewpoint
  • Experience the cool mountain climate

If You Ride There

  • Take Kennon Road or Marcos Highway from Baguio
  • Halsema Highway is the highest road in the Philippines — expect zigzag roads and cool temperatures
  • Bring a jacket — it gets cold at 1,500m elevation
  • Start early — the ride takes 6-7 hours from Manila
  • Consider overnight stay — it's too far for a day trip

⚠️ Rider Warning: Halsema Highway has steep cliffs and sharp curves. Only ride if you're experienced with mountain roads. Avoid during rainy season (June–October) due to landslide risk.
