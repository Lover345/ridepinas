Mayon Volcano

The World's Most Perfect Volcano

Back to Destinations

📍 Quick Facts

Location: Albay, Bicol
Distance: 500km from Manila
Ride Time: 9-10 hours
Road Type: SLEX + NLEX + Bicol Express
Best Time: November–May
Parking: Parking at Cagsawa Ruins

History

With its uniquely perfect cone shape, Mayon Volcano is one of the most recognizable natural landmarks in the Philippines. Located in Albay, Bicol Region, it has erupted over 50 times in the past 400 years.

The most devastating eruption was in 1814, which buried the town of Cagsawa and its church, leaving only the bell tower visible. Today, the Cagsawa Ruins are a popular tourist spot and a reminder of Mayon's power.

Why Visit?

  • See the world's most perfect volcanic cone — 2,462 meters high
  • Visit the Cagsawa Ruins — buried by the 1814 eruption
  • Go on an ATV trail around the volcano's base
  • Try Bicolano food — Bicol Express, laing, pili nuts
  • Experience the warmth of Bicolano hospitality

If You Ride There

  • Take SLEX → NLEX → South Luzon Expressway → Bicol Express Highway
  • The ride is 9-10 hours — start very early or break it into 2 days
  • Stop in Lucena or Naga City for rest and food
  • The Bicol Express highway is one of the best motorcycle routes in the country
  • Bring rain gear — Bicol is one of the wettest regions

⚠️ Volcano Alert: Mayon is an active volcano. Check PHIVOLCS alerts before visiting. Some areas may be restricted during heightened volcanic activity.
