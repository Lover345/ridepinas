Luzon

Vigan Heritage Town

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Back to Destinations

📍 Quick Facts

Location: Ilocos Sur
Distance: 408km from Manila
Ride Time: 7-8 hours
Road Type: NLEX + SCTEX + National Highway
Best Time: October–March
Parking: Public parking near the plaza

History

Vigan is one of the best-preserved Spanish colonial towns in Asia. Founded in 1574, it served as the capital of the Ilocos region during Spanish colonization. The city's historic center, known as the Mestizo District, showcases Spanish, Filipino, and Chinese architectural influences.

Calle Crisologo, the most famous street, is lined with ancestral houses dating back to the 18th century. Walking through its cobblestone streets feels like stepping back in time. In 1999, Vigan was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site for being the best-preserved example of a planned Spanish colonial town in Asia.

Why Visit?

  • Walk through Calle Crisologo — the most photographed street in the Philippines
  • Ride a kalesa (horse-drawn carriage) through the old streets
  • Visit the St. Paul's Metropolitan Cathedral
  • Explore the Syquia Mansion Museum
  • Try Vigan longganisa and empanada
  • Shop for local crafts and souvenir items

If You Ride There

  • Take NLEX → SCTEX → Tarlac → National Highway to Vigan
  • The ride is long (7-8 hours) — start early and take breaks in Tarlac or Pangasinan
  • Park outside the historic center and walk in
  • Best to visit during cooler months (October–March)
  • Bring a jacket — Ilocos can get cool, especially at night

💡 Rider Tip: Stop in Pangasinan for lunch — try the local bangus (milkfish) dishes. The milkfish capital of the Philippines!
